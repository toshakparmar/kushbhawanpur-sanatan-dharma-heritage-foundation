# Story Section Sources & Claims Reference

This document catalogs every scriptural, botanical, historical, and local gazetteer claim referenced in the **"Our Story — Five Symbols, One Sankalpa"** section of the Kushbhawanpur Sanatan Dharma & Heritage Foundation website.

Tone and wording policy: wherever the status is "Tradition" or marked with ⚑, the copy uses reverent attribution such as *"tradition holds"* or *"according to local accounts"*, without exaggerated or unverified empirical claims.
